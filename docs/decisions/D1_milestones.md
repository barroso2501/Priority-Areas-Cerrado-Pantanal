# D1 — Temporal milestones

**Status:** Open · **Raised:** 2026-10-02

## Context
Each assessment cycle has three dates: the data year that fed the selection, the workshop year and the legal-act year. "Before/after" results shift by 4–6 years depending on the choice. The MapBiomas annual series (1985–present) makes snapshots unnecessary.

| Cycle | Data year | Workshop | Legal act |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st update | ~2002 (PROBIO vegetation) | 2006 | Ordinance 9/2007 |
| 2nd update | 2009 remnants; 2002–2008 deforestation | 2011–2012 | Ordinance 223/2016; 463/2018 |

## Options
1. Data years only (state at selection time).
2. Act years only (policy-effect framing).
3. **Both, with distinct roles.** State at T0 = 2002, T2 = 2009 and T4 = last year. Attributable change from T1 = 2007 and T3 = 2016, with 2012–2016 as "announced, not enacted".

## Recommendation
Option 3, over the full annual series, with annualized rates per period.

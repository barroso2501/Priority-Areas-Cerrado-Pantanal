# D3 — Rule for fire as degradation

**Status:** Open · **Raised:** 2026-10-02

## Context
Fire belongs to the regime of Cerrado savannas and grasslands. Counting burned area as degradation penalizes well-conserved areas. Degradation is a departure from the expected regime.

## Options for the indicator
1. Burned area (rejected: confounds regime with degradation).
2. **Regime departure**, built from:
   - frequency above a physiognomy-specific threshold;
   - share of late-dry-season fire (e.g. Aug–Oct; exact window to be defined);
   - any fire in forest formations, veredas or gallery forests;
   - optionally, time since last fire.
3. Use the MapBiomas Degradation module's fire-frequency vector as-is.

## Recommendation
Option 2, with thresholds taken from the literature and the project's fire-seasonality work, and Option 3 as a cross-check.

## To define
Thresholds per physiognomy; seasonal window; treatment of the Pantanal (flood-dependent regime).

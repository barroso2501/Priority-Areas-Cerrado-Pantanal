# D4 — Cost

**Status:** D4 Accepted (2026-10-02) · D4b Open · D4c Open

## D4 — Definition (Accepted)
Cost = the spatial characteristics that entered the systematic-conservation-planning cost surface (constraints and opportunities for conservation), **not land price**. Decided by the project lead.

Reference surface (2nd update, WWF/MMA 2015 pp. 21–22, 36): three themes rescaled 0–10 and summed per planning unit:
1. deforestation probability (LCM, 2002→2008);
2. infrastructure density;
3. land price.

## D4b — Land-price component (Open)
The 2012 surface includes land price as one of its three components, and no public historical land-price series exists.

Options:
- (a) freeze at 2012 values;
- (b) omit, 2-component surface;
- (c) spatial proxy (agricultural suitability or rent).

**Recommendation:** (b) as the main analysis and (a) as sensitivity, so that cost variation is not mixed with a frozen component.

## D4c — Rescaling (Open)
The 0–10 rescaling is relative to each year's distribution; recomputing it per year re-centres cost and hides change.

**Recommendation:** fix the scale bounds at the reference year (2009/2012).

## Notes

- The 2012 planning-unit grid is unavailable; rebuild it from HydroSHEDS or summarize per area and control cell.
